# Decision: move-v1-public-launch-to-late-january

## What we decided

Option A from [[roadmap]] §3: the full V1 (milestones M0–M10, including subscriptions) ships as the public UK App Store release, now targeted for **late January 2027** instead of the end of November 2026.

Kyle will still work intensively and test how far better documentation speeds things up. Velocity is measured against the roadmap's estimates at a checkpoint after M2 (Slice 100, speckit 007). If actual effort is well under estimate (roughly 60% or less), an earlier closed beta (option B) is reconsidered. The January public date stays the baseline either way.

## Why

The full V1 is about 37–56 days of work against about 20–25 available before the end of November. Moving the date keeps subscriptions in the launch and avoids cutting the creative experience.

## Alternatives considered

- **B. Closed beta by the end of November, public in January:** kept as the fallback if the checkpoint shows high velocity.
- **C. Public launch at the end of November without subscriptions:** rejected as too risky.

## Trade-offs accepted

Two more months without public users or revenue.
