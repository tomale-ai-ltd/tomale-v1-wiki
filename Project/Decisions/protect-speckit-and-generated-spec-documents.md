# Decision: protect-speckit-and-generated-spec-documents

## What we decided

These documents stay where they are and are edited only through named processes. `constitution.md`: `/speckit.constitution` from an approved `repo-sync` item. `data-model.md`, `xano.yaml`, `client.ts`: generated only. `specs/*`: speckit commands. `specify-rules.mdc`: never edited (regenerated by `update-agent-context.sh`; fix content at its source, pin extras only in `MANUAL ADDITIONS`). `.specify/templates`, `.specify/scripts`, `.cursor/commands/speckit.*`: only by upgrading speckit. Published XanoScript: Xano only. Canonical vault docs: only through approved `pending-updates` or an in-session decision.

## Why

Cursor and the speckit scripts depend on these files in place; hand edits or moves would break the spec-driven flow or be overwritten.

## Alternatives considered

Move or copy specs into the vault for editing (rejected).

## Trade-offs accepted

Spec changes that come from chat go through a queue and a Cursor step.
