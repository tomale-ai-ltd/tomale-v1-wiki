## Goal

A parent or a child makes a story in a few taps: say what happens (or tap "Surprise me"), confirm who's in it and what kind of story it is (both suggested from the idea), choose how it looks, optionally a phonics focus, then "Make my story". They can leave while it's made, see it being made from Home and Create, get a "Your story is ready" toast, and open the finished story's cover. Unfinished stories wait in a Story builder so nothing is lost, including when the monthly limit is reached.

## Screen flow

- **Entry points** (all behind 010b's email lock, unchanged): Create tab "Make a story", Home "New story" / "Make a story", character detail "Create a story with [name]" and the character panel's "Make a story" (that character is already chosen), and a Story builder card (resumes that draft).
- **Create tab (1101):** the two tiles stay; below them **"Being made"** (stories in Preparing or In Queue, each with progress) and **"Story builder"** (the active profile's drafts: title or "Untitled story", up to three character pictures, theme name, "Edited 2 days ago"; tap resumes; the "…" menu has "Delete draft" with a confirmation). Each section is hidden when empty.
- **Story chat (1102):** one screen, compact header with the kit BackButton and "New story" (or the draft's title), "Step N of 6". Background: the cream page with faint scattered brand shapes (bubbles, dots and sparkles from the logo). Tomale speaks on the **left** in **grey** bubbles with the book mark avatar; the person's answers sit on the **right** in **coral** bubbles (`ink` text) with **their profile avatar** beside them. Tapping a card sends it as their reply. The whole conversation so far scrolls above the current question. Steps, in order:
  1. **What happens in your story?** A text box ("A dragon who's scared of the dark…", up to 300 characters), a "Give me ideas" chip (three idea cards from the AI: title and one line, built from the household's characters and this profile's recent stories; tapping one fills the box, which stays editable; "More ideas" asks again) and a **"Surprise me"** chip. "Next" confirms the idea: the server checks it first (moderation), so a refused idea stays here with a Tomale bubble ("Hmm, I can't make a story about that. Let's try a different idea."), and a possible disclosure opens the safe-message sheet (1109) instead of moving on. Surprise me: the AI picks an idea, one to three characters and a theme from this profile's recent stories and the household's characters; the art style is this profile's most recent story's style (else the first style); no phonics; the chat then jumps to the summary (step 6) so the person can check or change anything before "Make my story".
  2. **Who's in your story?** Household characters as picture cards (multi-select, 1 to 3), plus a "Make a new character" card that opens the 008 form and comes back with the new character chosen. Characters are **pre-ticked** from the idea: at once, any character whose name appears in the text; then the AI suggestion (e.g. "the dragon" → Pip) adds or confirms ticks when it returns. A name shared by several characters ticks only the **most recently made** one, never a long list. Pre-ticked cards say "From your idea"; the person can untick. "These ones" confirms.
  3. **What kind of story?** Theme cards (name and one line), single choice. The AI's suggested theme is **pre-selected and shown first** ("Suggested"); the rest keep their order.
  4. **How should it look?** Art-style cards with the sample picture, single choice.
  5. **Practise some sounds?** "Not this time" (default) or "Yes, let's practise". Yes → stage cards with plain labels, grouped by phase (e.g. "Phase 3 · Week 2: ai, ee, igh, oa"). Tomale Phonics is the only scheme, so there's no scheme step; if a second scheme is ever added, scheme cards come first.
  6. **Here's your story:** a summary card (idea, characters, theme, style, sounds) with "Change" on each row (jumps back to that step, then returns here), the allowance line for a parent ("3 of 4 stories left this month"; hidden for a child), and **"Make my story"**.
- **Being made (1103):** straight after "Make my story": the book mark with a gentle animation (Reduce Motion: still), "We're making your story", a progress line ("Writing the words…", then "Drawing the pictures: 5 of 13"), and "You can leave. We'll tell you when it's ready." with "Back to Home". Closing or leaving doesn't stop anything. Progress is animated with the kit's Animate React Native components ([[adopt-animatereactnative-components-via-the-kit]]): while the words are written, a soft pulsing ring around the book mark (no number); while pictures are drawn, the ring fills in 13 segments as each picture lands, with the count below. Reduce Motion: a still ring and the text only.
- **Being-made card (1104)** on Home (parent and child) and in Create: cover placeholder, "Being made", the same progress line; tap opens 1103 for that story. The cover placeholder is the Tomale book mark on a soft teal tile.
- **Pull to refresh** on Home, Create and the story lists: pulling down stretches a small book mark, it hangs (gently bobbing, sparkles circling) while the lists reload, then springs back. Reduce Motion: the plain system indicator. It reloads the in-progress list, the Story builder and the story list, and doesn't replace the automatic checks.
- **Ready (1105):** while the app is open, a "Your story is ready" toast (with the title) above the tab bar, with "Open". The story appears in Home's list as normal. A child's story held for a grown-up becomes 010's waiting card instead, and the child's toast says "Your story is made! A grown-up will look at it soon." (no "Open"). This is a moment to celebrate, so the toast is richer than the 010 confirmation toast: a card with the story's cover thumbnail, "Your story is ready!" and the title, springing up from the tab bar with a short sparkle burst from the book mark; tap anywhere on it (or "Open") to open the story; swipe down or sideways to dismiss; it stays 8 seconds, with a thin line along the bottom showing the time left, and pauses while touched. Reduce Motion: fades in, no sparkles. Only one at a time: two stories ready together show "2 stories are ready" and open Home.
- **Story cover (1106):** cover picture, title, subtitle, characters, the phonics focus if any ("Practising: Phase 3, week 2"), and a disabled "Read" with "Reading comes soon" (012 replaces this screen's button with the reader). When a phonics focus was chosen, a **"Sounds in this story"** section shows the stage ("Tomale Phonics · Phase 3, week 2"), that stage's graphemes as chips (with an example word each where the data has one) and its tricky words as chips, with a line saying the story also uses the sounds from earlier stages. The same section goes on the story detail in 012.
- **Limit reached (1107):** a sheet on "Make my story" when the cap is hit; the story stays in the Story builder. Child: "You've made lots of stories this month! New ones unlock on [1 December]." with "Ask a grown-up" (opens the profile picker with the parent highlighted, then the PIN, then parent Home; no prices) and "OK". Parent: "You've used this month's [4] stories. More unlock on [1 December]." with OK (plans and upgrades come with M8).
- **Couldn't make it (1108):** a moderation block or a failed generation. Blocked (child and parent): "We can't make this story. Let's try a different idea." with "Try a different idea" (opens a new chat with the same characters, theme and style chosen, the idea box empty). Failed (story rolled back to Draft): "Something went wrong making your story." with "Try again" (back to the summary) and "Later" (it stays in the Story builder). Never say why a story was blocked to a child.
- **Something worrying (1109):** when the idea, at any point, reads like a child (or adult) telling us something is wrong in their life (being hurt, touched, frightened, wanting to hurt themselves), no story is made and a calm sheet opens. Child: "It sounds like something might be worrying you. Talking to a grown-up you trust can really help. You can also talk to Childline. It's free, and they're there any time." with "Call Childline: 0800 1111", "childline.org.uk" and "OK". Adult profile: "It sounds like things might be hard right now. You don't have to deal with it alone." with Samaritans (116 123, free, any time) and "OK". No blame, no "try again" pressure, nothing else on screen. The parent is **not** told automatically (the person hurting a child may be in the household); Tomale reviews it (see the Xano prompts). After OK, the chat stays at step 1 with an empty idea box.

## Scope (this slice only)

- **Draft and conversation:** opening the chat creates the draft and its conversation at once: `POST /instant_stories {}` returns the story with `conversation_id` (the server creates the `conversations` row, `conversation_type` story, linked by `instant_stories_id`). A draft with no confirmed idea is hidden from the Story builder (and cleaned up by the backend later). Confirming the idea is `POST /instant_stories/{id}/idea {user_prompt}`: the server moderates it, saves it and returns `{characters_ids, theme_id}` suggestions; refusals by code: `CONTENT_REJECTED` → the Tomale bubble at step 1, `SAFEGUARDING_CONCERN` → 1109. Characters save with `POST /instant_stories/{id}/characters {characters_ids}`; later steps with `PATCH /instant_stories/{id}` (`theme_id`, `artwork_style_id`, `phonics_phase_stage_id`; the idea only changes through `…/idea`, so every version is moderated). Back, closing the app or a dropped connection lose nothing already confirmed.
- **Conversation log (server-written):** every story endpoint appends an event to the story's conversation as it runs (idea versions, AI ideas and suggestions offered, Surprise me picks, character, theme, style and phonics choices, moderation outcomes, "Make my story"), with time, step and profile. The app never writes the log. **Resuming** renders the chat from `GET /instant_stories/{id}/conversation` (the safe, display-only events: questions are drawn from the step, answers from the event), then opens at the first unanswered step, or the summary if all are done. Moderation and safeguarding details are never returned to the app.
- **Catalogues:** `GET /themes?status=active` (check the live value), `GET /artwork_style`, `GET /characters` (household, not archived), `GET /phonics/phonics_schemes` and `…/{id}/stages`. Loaded when the chat opens; a failed load shows the step's "Try again" bubble. Locked art styles (plans) are M8: every live style is selectable in 011.
- **AI help:** `POST /instant_stories/{id}/ideas` → `{ideas: [{title, idea}]}` (three, from the household's characters and this profile's recent stories); `POST /instant_stories/{id}/surprise` → `{user_prompt, characters_ids, theme_id, artwork_style_id}`, saved on the draft by the server (moderated like a typed idea), then the chat jumps to the summary. Suggestions come back from `…/idea` (above). The name match runs in the app at once (case-insensitive whole words; a shared name ticks the most recently made character); the server's suggestions add or confirm ticks. Typing dots while waiting; `RATE_LIMITED` → "That's lots of ideas! Try writing your own, or ask again in a minute."; any other failure → "Ideas aren't working right now. You can write your own."
- **Make my story:** `GET /story_allowance` when the summary opens (`{used, limit, resets_at}`; parent line only). "Make my story" → `POST /instant_stories/{id}/page_text`. Navigate to 1103 at once and don't wait for the response (text generation can take a minute); the screen follows `GET /instant_stories/{id}`. The button can't be tapped twice.
- **Refusals by `payload.code`:** `ENTITLEMENT_LIMIT_REACHED` (with `payload.resets_at`) → 1107; `EMAIL_NOT_VERIFIED` → 010b's confirm sheet (the in-app lock normally prevents this); `STORY_NOT_EDITABLE` → reload the story and open 1103 or the cover for its real status; `STORY_NOT_FOUND` → back to Create with "That story isn't here any more"; `INVALID_CHARACTERS` → reload characters and ask step 2 again; `INVALID_INPUT` → reload the catalogues and ask that step again; `CONTENT_REJECTED` and `SAFEGUARDING_CONCERN` as above (also possible from `…/surprise` and `page_text`).
- **Following progress:** poll `GET /instant_stories/{id}` every 10 s while 1103 is open, and the in-progress list (`GET /instant_stories?in_progress=true`) every 30 s while Home or Create is focused and the list isn't empty; stop in the background; check once on return to the foreground. Progress text: Preparing → "Writing the words…"; In Queue → "Drawing the pictures: (total − images_remaining) of total". Done → the ready toast once per story per device session (a set of story ids in memory), then the story leaves the in-progress list. Blocked → 1108 (a story blocked for a safeguarding reason shows 1109 instead: `GET /instant_stories/{id}` returns `blocked_kind` "content" or "safeguarding", never the reason). Draft again after "Make my story" → the failed state of 1108.
- **Toast:** reuse the shared confirmation toast host (010b). "Open" goes to 1106 (or, for a child's held story, there is no "Open").
- **Story cover (1106):** `GET /instant_stories/{id}`; the e-reader is 012.
- **Story builder:** `GET /instant_stories?status=Draft` (the active profile's own drafts, newest first); delete with `DELETE /instant_stories/{id}`.
- **Phonics:** stored as `phonics_phase_stage_id`; stages from `GET /phonics/phonics_schemes/{id}/stages` for the only scheme; the summary shows the stage's plain label; the cover's section uses `phonics` on `GET /instant_stories/{id}` (`{scheme, phase, stage, graphemes: [{grapheme, example}], tricky_words}`).
- **Pull to refresh:** Home, Create and the story lists, as described above.
- **Child and parent:** the same flow and copy, written so a 5-year-old can follow it with help. No prices, plans or purchase prompts for a child anywhere (CAP Code; [[pre-launch-checklist]] §6).
- **Copy** follows the design-system README and British English; "story", never "book" (books are the printed product in the Store).

## Design notes (for the mock-ups)

- Chat: Tomale's bubbles left, grey (`surface-sunken`) with `ink` text and the book mark avatar; the person's replies right, coral with `ink` text and their profile avatar; cards in a wrapped grid (characters, themes, styles, stages), 48-point targets, a tick on a chosen card, "From your idea" / "Suggested" tags. Typing dots (S-032's three pulsing dots) while the AI works. The text box appears only at step 1. Cream background with faint brand shapes behind the conversation, never under text.
- Being made: an animated ring around the book mark (pulsing while writing; 13 segments filling while drawing) and the count; no percentage.
- Ready toast: a card with the cover thumbnail, spring entrance, sparkle burst, tap to open, swipe to dismiss, an 8-second time-left line.
- Pull to refresh: a stretching book mark that hangs with circling sparkles, then springs back.
- Safe-message sheet (1109): calm, plain, no illustration that could read as playful; large tap targets for the helpline; nothing behind it changes.
- Sheets keep the 40% `ink` scrim, no blur. Header bands, BackButton and `ScreenHeader` as in 009/010b.
- All motion uses the kit's Animate React Native components and respects Reduce Motion.

## Out of scope

- The e-reader and the Bookshelf tab (012); the alerts inbox (M7) and push notifications (post-V1); plans, upgrades and locked art styles (M8); AI-led free chat (guided steps only, Kyle 24/11); showing the conversation log to anyone but Tomale's review (the app only shows the person their own chat when resuming); automatic account locks from strikes (Tomale reviews; see the Xano prompts); naming the story yourself (the generation writes the title); editing a story after "Make my story"; regenerating pictures (V2); 16- and 20-page stories; draft clean-up (a backend job, see the Xano prompts).

## State Management

`store/storyDraftStore.ts` (Zustand, memory only): the open draft's fields, the current step, catalogue caches for the session, the conversation events loaded on resume, and the ideas and suggestions returned. `store/storyProgressStore.ts` (memory only): in-progress stories `{id, status, total_pages, images_remaining}`, the polling timers and the set of ids already toasted. Nothing persisted: the server is the record (Story builder and the in-progress list are refetched on focus). Both stores are cleared with the other household caches on sign-out, Start again and a new sign-in (010b bug fix `ca572a0`).

## Data the app needs (from [[011-xano-prompts]])

- `PATCH /instant_stories/{id}` (Draft only), `POST /instant_stories/{id}/characters` (Draft only, 1–3 household characters, replaces the set), `POST /instant_stories {}` returning `conversation_id`, `POST /instant_stories/{id}/idea`, `POST /instant_stories/{id}/ideas`, `POST /instant_stories/{id}/surprise`, `GET /instant_stories/{id}/conversation`, `GET /story_allowance`, `GET /instant_stories?status=Draft` scoped to the active profile, `GET /instant_stories?in_progress=true` with `total_pages` and `images_remaining`, `phonics_phase_stage_id` on the story (create, patch) and `phonics` detail on get, the email gate and the monthly cap on `page_text`.
- New codes for the constitution's live list (a `/speckit.constitution` PATCH before plan): `STORY_NOT_EDITABLE`, `INVALID_CHARACTERS`, `INVALID_INPUT`, `SAFEGUARDING_CONCERN`, `ENTITLEMENT_LIMIT_REACHED` (moves from planned to live). `STORY_NOT_FOUND`, `EMAIL_NOT_VERIFIED`, `RATE_LIMITED` and `CONTENT_REJECTED` are already live.
