Tomale Content Moderation Policy V1.0

1. Purpose:

Tomale is a children’s storytelling platform. All content must be safe, age-appropriate, emotionally responsible, and original. This policy governs how user prompts are assessed before story generation. 

Our moderation systems classify content, determine whether it may proceed, and apply appropriate safeguards. The goal is prevention, proportionality, and child safety, not censorship for its own sake.

2. Moderation Outcomes:

All content must be classified into one of the following outcomes

- PASS

Content is safe and appropriate. Story generation and viewing may proceed without restriction.

- PARENTAL OVERSIGHT

Content is sensitive but may be appropriate with adult awareness. Story generation may proceed, but access to the resulting story must be gated behind parental controls in the user interface.

- FAIL

Content is disallowed. Story generation must not proceed. Some specified FAIL outcomes may also require silent account flagging and internal escalation.


3. Assessment Criteria


a.) Violence & Conflict

PASS — Non-graphic, non-lethal, non-cruel conflict is allowed when appropriate for children, including:

Stylised fantasy battles, magical duels, sporting or rule-based combat (e.g. boxing, fencing, martial arts training), clear “good vs wrong” moral framing.

Conditions:

No blood or gore, no focus on injury or pain, no death, no cruelty, no realistic depictions of suffering.

Examples:

A hero fighting a monster and scaring it away, a sports match with respect and sportsmanship, non-violent physical conflict between protagonist & antagonist resulting in defeat.

PARENTAL OVERSIGHT — Sensitive conflict allowed only with parental awareness:

Fantasy peril that creates tension, brief, non-graphic references to someone being hurt, fear or danger that is resolved safely and positively.

Constraints:

Must not dwell on pain or fear, must resolve with safety, learning, or reassurancem, must not glorify harm.

FAIL — Disallowed forms of violence:

Graphic violence or injury. Blood, gore, broken bones. Killing or death as a goal or reward. Torture or cruelty. Realistic modern weapon violence.

Guiding principle: If the appeal of the scene depends on injury, pain, or fear, it is not allowed.


b.) Hate, Bullying & Discrimination

PASS - Stories promoting kindness, empathy, inclusion, or conflict resolution.

PARENTAL OVERSIGHT - Mild bullying & interpersonal conflict only if clearly framed as wrong or problematic. Consequences are shown. The story resolves with learning or repair.

FAIL - Disallowed forms of hate, bullying & discrimination:

Hate speech. Racism, sexism, or discrimination. Targeting protected characteristics. Any form of bullying, hate or discrimination presented as acceptable, humorous, or rewarding.


c.) Sexual Content & Child Safety - Zero Tolerance:

FAIL (Automatic) - Any sexual content involving minors. Sexualised descriptions of children. Grooming behaviour. Sexual innuendo or euphemisms involving children. “Dogwhistles” or coded sexual language. Attempts to test boundaries around child sexuality

** There is no parental oversight for this category **

The system must:

Block generation, avoid alerting the user to internal actions, flag the account internally for review


d.) Sensitive Topics

PARENTAL OVERSIGHT - Allowed only with care:

Grief or loss (e.g. death of a pet, family member or friend) where life is valued & celebrated. Illness or emotional struggle showed with hope and potential resolution. Fear or sadness handled gently and with empathy.

Constraints: Non-graphic depictions. Remain emotionally supportive and provide an age-appropriate resolution. 

FAIL - Abuse, war, extreme poverty, drug or substance abuse, self-harm or suicide. Real-world trauma presented realistically


e.) Illegal or Unethical Behaviour

FAIL - Crime, theft, or illegal acts glorifyed, or portrayed as acceptable or admirable traits. Deception or manipulation without clear consequences.

Wrongdoing may only appear if:

It is clearly framed as wrong. Consequences and moral lessons are explicit


f.) Copyright Infringement and Intellectual Property

PASS - Child-led imaginative stories that use unique or familiar ideas in new ways without seeking to recreate or depend on the specific details of existing fictional works.

The following are allowed:

- Generic story arcs, mythic structures, and familiar tropes
- Archetypal roles (heroes, villains, mentors, rivals)
- Imaginative play inspired by popular culture
- Original characters, even if stylistically inspired
- Common cultural terms used in a non-specific, creative way
- Stories should be reasonably understandable and enjoyable without requiring explicit knowledge of a specific, existing copyrighted material.
- Fan-fiction, or stories inspired by popular culture with independent narratives, characters and settings.

FAIL - Specific and purposeful replication of specific stories, locations, characters or items from third-party copyrighted materials

Stories must not:

- Use distinctive proper nouns from known fictional works (e.g. specific character names, planet names, organisations, unique weapons)
- Explicitly place the story within a named fictional universe
- Recreate identifiable characters, locations, or events from a copyrighted work
- Depend on recognition of a specific third-party franchise to make sense

Examples of disallowed content:

- Named characters or locations from existing franchises
- Retelling or continuation of a specific film, book, or TV storyline
- Stories that function primarily as substitutes for an existing work


4. Silent Account Flagging


- Our moderation systems will internally flag accounts without notifying the user in cases involving:

Child sexual exploitation or abusive content. Repeated attempts to bypass moderation. Use of known abusive or sexualised dogwhistles. Persistent requests for disallowed material. Attempts to generate copyrighted characters after prior blocks

- Flagging enables internal safeguards such as evidence preservation and review, the details of these processes are intentionally not exposed to users.
- **Strikes (26/11):** each refused idea or story is a strike; 3 strikes in 30 days flags the account for internal review. The user is never told.

5. Safeguarding concern (26/11)
- When text reads like the writer (a child or an adult) disclosing that something harmful is happening to them, or that they may hurt themselves, the outcome is `safeguarding_concern`, not FAIL. This is about the person telling us something about their own life, not a story theme.
- No story is made. The person sees a calm safe message (Childline for a child, Samaritans for an adult). It is not a strike, the parent is not alerted automatically (the person causing harm may be in the household), and Tomale reviews it internally under the safeguarding runbook.


5. Enforcement Rules


- Moderation occurs before generation
- Classification must be clear and proportional
- If content cannot be confidently classified → FAIL
- Parental oversight allows generation but restricts viewing
- FAIL always blocks generation
- Serious child-safety violations are flagged for review without user feedback


6. Final Platform Position


- Tomale prioritises child safety, emotional responsibility, and originality. We allow imagination and adventure without harm, fear or exploitation.
