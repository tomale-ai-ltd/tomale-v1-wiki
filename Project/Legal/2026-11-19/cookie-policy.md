# Tomale Cookie Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser so it can remember you between pages or visits. This policy explains the cookies on tomale.ai and how to choose which ones you allow.

- **The Tomale app doesn't use cookies or tracking.** It keeps your sign-in in your phone's secure storage and uses no advertising or analytics trackers.
- **On our website, we ask first.** Only cookies the site needs to work are set straight away. Analytics and advertising cookies are set only if you click "Accept" on our cookie banner, and you can change your mind at any time.
- How we use any personal information collected this way is explained in our [Privacy Notice](https://www.tomale.ai/privacy-notice).

## The cookies we use

| Type | Cookie | Set by | What it does | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Necessary (always on) | `crumb` | Squarespace | Protects forms on the site from misuse | Until you close the browser |
| Necessary (always on) | Your cookie choice | Squarespace | Remembers whether you accepted or declined cookies | Up to 1 year |
| Analytics (only if you accept) | `ss_cvr`, `ss_cvt`, `ss_cid` and similar | Squarespace | Counts visits and pages viewed, so we can see how the site is used | Up to 2 years |
| Analytics (only if you accept) | `_ga`, `_ga_<ID>` | Google Analytics | Tells visits apart and measures how people use the site | Up to 2 years |
| Advertising (only if you accept) | `_gcl_au` and Google advertising cookies (on google.com and doubleclick.net) | Google | Measures whether our Google adverts lead to visits and sign-ups, and lets us show Tomale adverts to people who visited (remarketing) | Up to 13 months |
| Advertising (only if you accept) | `_fbp`, `fr` | Meta (Facebook and Instagram) | Measures whether our adverts on Meta lead to visits and sign-ups, and lets us show Tomale adverts to people who visited (remarketing) | Up to 90 days |

The pages that confirm your email or open the app after a sign-in link set only necessary cookies. We don't use cookies to show adverts on our own site or in the app.

## Your choices

- **On our site:** use the cookie banner, or the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of any page, to accept or decline analytics and advertising cookies. Declining doesn't stop the site working.
- **In your browser:** you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may stop parts of the site working.
- **Google and Meta:** Google offers an [Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), and Meta lets you manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

If we add or change cookies, we'll update this page and its "Last updated" date, and ask for your choice again where needed. Questions: support@tomale.ai, or Tomale.ai Ltd, 12 Knutsford Road, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 6JA.
