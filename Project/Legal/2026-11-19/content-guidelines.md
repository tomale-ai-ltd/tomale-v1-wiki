# Tomale Content Guidelines

Last updated 19 November 2026

Tomale is for stories that are safe, kind and made for children. Adventure, mischief, big feelings and baddies are all welcome; harm, fear for its own sake, and copying other people's stories are not. These guidelines explain what our checks look for, so grown-ups and children know what to expect.

## How the checks work

Every story request and every story's text is checked automatically before the story is shown. Each one ends in one of three outcomes:

| Outcome | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Good to go | The story is made and the child can read it straight away. |
| A grown-up takes a look | The story is made, but a child sees "Waiting for a grown-up" until a parent reads it and approves it. The parent is emailed and sees it in Approvals. |
| Not made | The story isn't made. We suggest trying a different idea. |

When our checks aren't sure, they choose the safer outcome. Character pictures and any extra drawing instructions are checked the same way; because characters are shared across the household, they're either fine or not drawn.

## What we look for

The rule of thumb: if a scene only works because someone is hurt, frightened or treated unkindly, it isn't for Tomale.

| Topic | Welcome | A grown-up takes a look | Not made |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adventure and conflict | Heroes scaring off monsters, magical duels, sports and martial arts with good sportsmanship, baddies who are beaten | Peril and tension, or someone getting a little hurt, as long as it ends safely and kindly | Blood, gore or serious injury; death or killing as the goal; cruelty; realistic modern weapons |
| Kindness and bullying | Friendship, empathy, including others, sorting out disagreements | Mild bullying or falling out, shown as wrong, with consequences and making up | Hate, discrimination, or bullying presented as funny, fine or rewarded |
| Big feelings and hard topics | Worries and sadness handled gently | Losing a pet or a loved one, illness or a hard time, told with care, hope and a gentle ending | Abuse, war, drugs, self-harm or suicide; real-world trauma told realistically |
| Right and wrong | Mischief that's clearly mischief | Wrongdoing that's clearly wrong, with consequences and a lesson | Crime, stealing or tricking people shown as clever, admirable or without consequences |
| Child safety |  |  | Anything sexual involving children, or that looks like it's testing those limits. There's no grown-up review for this: it is never made. |

## Original stories

Tomale makes new stories. Being inspired by favourite books, films and games is fine; copying them isn't.

- **Welcome:** classic story shapes (quests, rescues, mysteries), heroes, villains, mentors and rivals, dragons, wizards, space explorers and superheroes of your own, and your own characters, even in the style of something you love.
- **Not made:** named characters, places, teams or objects from existing books, films, TV shows or games; stories set in a named fictional world; and retellings or sequels of a particular story.
- **A quick test:** if the story would make sense to someone who has never seen the film or read the book, it's probably fine.

## When a story isn't made

- **Try again:** most blocked ideas work with a small change, such as a new name for a character or a gentler ending.
- **Repeated attempts:** trying again and again to get round the checks, or to make content that's never allowed, may lead us to review, limit or close the account ([Terms](https://www.tomale.ai/terms), section 6). Where the law requires it, or a child may be at risk, we report content to the police or other authorities.
- **Checks aren't perfect:** they can sometimes hold a story that's fine, or miss something that isn't. Please read stories with younger children.
- **Tell us:** if something got through that shouldn't have, or a story was held or blocked by mistake, email support@tomale.ai.
