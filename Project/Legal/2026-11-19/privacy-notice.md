# Tomale Privacy Notice

Last updated 19 November 2026

## 1. Who we are and the short version

Tomale.ai Ltd ("Tomale", "we") decides how your information is used, so we are its controller under UK data protection law. We're a company registered in England and Wales, number 15649175, at 12 Knutsford Road, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 6JA. For anything about your privacy, email support@tomale.ai.

This notice covers the Tomale app, the website at tomale.ai, and printed book orders. The short version:

- **We collect only what Tomale needs:** your account details, the profiles you create, the stories and characters your household makes, and order details when you buy a book.
- **A grown-up is always in charge.** Children use Tomale through profiles on a parent's account. We don't ask children for their email, age or surname.
- **We don't sell your information, show adverts in the app, or use your stories, pictures or children's details to train AI models.**
- **We use AI services to write and draw stories,** and automatic checks to keep them suitable for children.
- **You're in control:** you can see, change or delete your information, and close your account at any time.

## 3. Children's information

Tomale is made for families, so we take extra care with children's information, following the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code.

- **What we hold about a child:** the profile name and picture a parent chooses, and the stories and characters made on that profile. We don't ask for a child's age, date of birth, surname, school or email.
- **A parent decides:** only the account holder (18 or over) can create, change or delete a child's profile, and a parental PIN protects those settings. Stories that need a grown-up's look are held until a parent approves them.
- **Private by default:** stories stay within your household. There are no public profiles, chat with other users, adverts or profiling of children.
- **Real details:** a story can include anything typed into it, such as a child's name, a pet or a favourite place. Please don't include addresses, school names or health details; our checks may hold stories that do.
- **Children's rights:** a child has the same rights over their information as an adult (section 8). A parent can use them for a younger child.

UK data protection law says we need a legal reason for each use. We use your information only for these purposes:

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we've weighed them against your rights, with children's interests first. You can object (section 8).

## 5. AI and content checks

Tomale uses AI services to write story text, draw pictures and create read-aloud audio from what you type. To do that, we send the request, character names and descriptions, and the story so far to our AI provider, OpenAI. We don't send your email address, payment details or delivery address.

- **No training:** we don't use your content to train AI models, and our agreement with OpenAI's API stops them using it to train theirs.
- **Automatic checks:** every request and story is checked automatically before anyone sees it. A check can let a story through, hold it for a parent, or stop it being made (see the [Content Guidelines](https://www.tomale.ai/content-guidelines)).
- **Decisions with a person in them:** a held story is decided by you, not by the system. If a check stops a story or affects your account and you think it's wrong, email support@tomale.ai and a person will look at it.
- **Serious concerns:** requests that suggest a child may be at risk are kept, reviewed by a person and, where the law requires or a child may be in danger, reported to the police or other authorities.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open, and delete it on the timetable below once it's no longer needed.

| Information | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Account, profiles, stories and characters | Until you close your account, then 30 days in case you come back; then deleted |
| A story, character or profile you delete | Removed from the app straight away; deleted from our systems within 30 days |
| Orders, payments and refunds | 6 years after the order, as tax law requires |
| Records of requests that broke our Content Guidelines | 2 years, including after an account closes, to protect children; longer only if the police or a court needs them |
| Crash reports | 90 days |
| Emails with our support team | 2 years after the last message |
| Marketing consent | Until you opt out, then a record that you opted out |

Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, so deleted information can take up to 35 days more to leave them.

## 8. Security, your rights and contact

- **Keeping it safe:** information travels encrypted, passwords and PINs are stored scrambled, sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage, and only the account's own profiles can see its stories. No system is perfect; if a breach puts you at risk, we'll tell you and the ICO as the law requires.
- **Your rights:** you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, move it to another service, limit how we use it, or object to a use based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent to marketing at any time with the unsubscribe link in any marketing email or at tomale.ai/unsubscribe. Messages about your account, approvals and orders aren't marketing, so they continue while you have an account; you can turn off notifications in your phone's settings. Email support@tomale.ai; we'll reply within one month, free of charge, and may ask you to confirm who you are.
- **In the app:** you can change names and pictures, delete stories, characters and profiles, and (once available) close your account in Settings.
- **Complaints:** please tell us first at support@tomale.ai. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or on 0303 123 1113.
- **Cookies:** the app doesn't use cookies or tracking. Our website uses cookies for analytics and to measure our adverts, only if you accept them; this is explained in our [Cookie Policy](https://www.tomale.ai/cookie-policy).
- **Changes:** we'll update the "Last updated" date when this notice changes, and tell you in the app or by email before an important change.
- **Contact:** Tomale.ai Ltd, 12 Knutsford Road, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 6JA, or support@tomale.ai.
