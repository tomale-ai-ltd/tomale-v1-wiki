# PU-2026-11-12-03: PRD-Characters, detail screen and confirmations

- Proposed change: scope line "Character detail page (read-only)" and the user flow (steps 4–8): one detail screen for both profiles, opened by a grid tap or "See more" on the short carousel panel (actions plus up to 2 stories; closes by card tap, Close or swipe). The parent sees More (archive, delete); the child sees the same screen without More, and favourite and Draw again work for the child. After archive or delete the parent returns to the list in use, with a toast "[Name] archived" / "[Name] deleted" over the tab bar (about 3 seconds, no undo; restore is Unarchive from the archived filter). Favourite, unfavourite, archive and unarchive are optimistic with rollback; delete is confirmed, then server. Search and filters apply to the grid only; the carousel shows active characters, favourites first. Check whether the long-press quick actions in the PRD still stand (the 008 spec doesn't mention them). Source: [[2026-11-12-001]] (008 clarify, analyze and device testing, Kyle 12/11).
