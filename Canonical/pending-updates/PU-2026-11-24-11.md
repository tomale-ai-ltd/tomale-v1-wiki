# PU-2026-11-24-11: purge never-confirmed accounts

Why: in 010b, "Wrong email? Start again" on Check your email revokes the session and returns to Create account; the Xano `users` row (and its primary profile) stays, unconfirmed, forever. Mistyped addresses and abandoned sign-ups accumulate, and a mistyped address can't be reused until its row goes.

- **[[pre-launch-checklist]] §1:** after the "Account closing and deletion" line, add: "- [ ] **Purge never-confirmed accounts:** the daily purge job also hard-deletes accounts with `email_verified` false created more than 7 days ago, with their profiles and sessions (they can't have orders, payments or stories, because creating needs a confirmed email). Postman case: an unconfirmed account older than the cutoff is gone after the job runs; a confirmed one is untouched."
- **[[backend-xano]]:** after the `purge_closed_accounts` sentence, add: "The same task deletes accounts still unconfirmed (`email_verified` false) 7 days after `created_at`, with their profiles and sessions."
- **[[roadmap]] M7, Account closing bullet:** add at the end: "The purge also removes accounts never confirmed after 7 days."

No reminder email (Kyle, 24/11): someone who hasn't confirmed in a week has no data and can sign up again.
