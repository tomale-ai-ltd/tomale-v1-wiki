## Decisions made

Candidates for decision notes in `Project/Decisions/`:

- **30-minute cancellation window before HPBC submission (1 min for test users).** Submission moves out of the `order_fulfilment_check` DB trigger into the `hpbc_submit_due_orders` background task gated on `submit_after`. This also resolves the "synchronous HPBC call inside a DB trigger" risk.
- **Personalised orders can't be cancelled once in production.** The customer approves a web-view preview before paying, and checkout states that orders can't be cancelled once in production. This relies on the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 reg. 28(1)(b) exemption for personalised goods; the terms wording needs checking. Accepted risk: the approval is based on the web view, not the PDF.
- **Self-service cancel = automatic full refund.** It is implemented under [[split-refund-model-into-self-service-and-support-paths]], with the idempotency key `refund_{order}_{refund_attempt_count}`, a retry task, and Stripe refund webhooks. Refunds made by hand in the Stripe dashboard are recorded but never touch `refundable_amount`.
- **HPBC callback status transitions are forward-only** (guard rules as above). There is no new Print Ready status: In Production maps to Pureprint's Order Print Ready.
- **No customer-facing status for Component Printed.**
- **Order Error on HPBC rejection**, and the lock is cleared on every failure path. Proposed (14b): failed checks and timeouts also go to Error rather than retrying.
- **Temporary `audit_store_orders` trigger as a diagnostic.** Whether to keep it as a permanent audit trail, with a retention period, is decided in the Phase 2 observability work. It holds shipping personal data, so it stays internal with no endpoint.
- **Documentation refresh happens after Phase 1 sign-off and before Cursor frontend work.**
- **Request history is off on functions that send credentials.**
